To use this module:

1.  Configure the delivery zones and their schedules in
    *Inventory \> Configuration \> Delivery Zones*.
2.  Assign a delivery zone to each partner.
3.  Go to *Settings \> General Settings \> Inventory* and set the
    **Departure Cutoff** (in minutes) — orders confirmed within this window
    before a departure are rolled to the next available slot.
4.  Confirm a sales order. The **Delivery Schedule** and **Scheduled Date**
    fields are automatically filled with the next reachable departure.
