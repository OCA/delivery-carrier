Once you configured you delivery fees setup, you can start applying them.

1. Place a new sales order.
1. Choose a delivery method with delivery fees configures.
1. Confirm the order.
1. Deliver the products.
1. Once the delivery picking is done, the delivey fees line will be added to the sale.
1. In the delivery slip, a message will be printed advising about these extra charges.
1. When the fees are invoiced, the same advicing message will be printed in the printed
   invoice as well.
