Once you configured your delivery fees setup, you can start applying them.

1. Place a new sales order.
1. Choose a delivery method with delivery fees configured.
1. Confirm the order.
1. Deliver the products.
1. Once the delivery picking is done, the delivery fees line will be added to the sale.
1. In the delivery slip, a message will be printed advising about these extra charges.
1. When the fees are invoiced, the same advisory message will be printed in the
   invoice as well.
