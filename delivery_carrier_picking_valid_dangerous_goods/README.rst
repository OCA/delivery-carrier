This is a glue module between delivery_carrier_picking_valid and
l10n_eu_product_adr_dangerous_goods. It allows to validate an order or
picking against a carrier regarding dangerous goods restrictions.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

To configure dangerous goods restrictions on a carrier, go on a given
carrier form, find the Availability tab on the notebook, and add the
desired limited amount on the ``ADR Limited Amount`` field. This will
restrict orders and pickings to those who doesn't have a product with
this flag in them.
