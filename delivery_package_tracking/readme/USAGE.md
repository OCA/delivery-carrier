On the delivery, put the goods of each parcel in a package (*Put in Pack*),
then open the *Packages* button and type the *Tracking Reference* of each
parcel; the field is also on the package form. The *Tracking Reference* of the
delivery becomes the list of the references of its packages, separated by
commas. A delivery without packages keeps the reference typed by hand.

Each package shows its own tracking link, built from the *Tracking Link* of
the carrier, and the *Tracking* button of the delivery posts the links of all
its packages in the chatter, as it does for the carrier integrations that
ship several parcels. In a multi-step delivery only the outgoing step checks
the references: pick and pack steps are not concerned.
