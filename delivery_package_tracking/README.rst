A delivery shipped in several parcels gets one tracking reference per
parcel from the carrier. Odoo keeps a single *Tracking Reference* on the
transfer, so today those deliveries are either split into one transfer
per parcel, which spoils the traceability of the order, or the
references are typed as one comma separated text nobody can search or
report on.

This module puts the tracking reference on the package: the warehouse
puts the goods of each parcel in a package, types the reference of the
parcel on it, and the transfer shows the references of all its packages,
as the carrier integrations do when they ship several parcels. A carrier
can require a tracking reference on every package before the delivery is
validated.

Configuration
=============

On the carriers that ship several parcels per delivery (*Inventory >
Configuration > Delivery Methods*), tick *Tracking Reference per
Package* to refuse the validation of a delivery while any of its
packages has no reference. Packages must be enabled in the inventory
settings. Fill in the *Tracking Link* of the carrier with
``<shipmenttrackingnumber>`` as placeholder to get a tracking link per
package.

Usage
=====

On the delivery, put the goods of each parcel in a package (*Put in
Pack*), then open the *Packages* button and type the *Tracking
Reference* of each parcel; the field is also on the package form. The
*Tracking Reference* of the delivery becomes the list of the references
of its packages, separated by commas. A delivery without packages keeps
the reference typed by hand.

Each package shows its own tracking link, built from the *Tracking Link*
of the carrier, and the *Tracking* button of the delivery posts the
links of all its packages in the chatter, as it does for the carrier
integrations that ship several parcels. In a multi-step delivery only
the outgoing step checks the references: pick and pack steps are not
concerned.
